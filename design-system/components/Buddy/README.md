# Buddy

Buddy is the crash-test teddy who guides every screen: honey fur, a sunshine helmet with a crash-test target, and a sky-blue Safety Shield.

**Provide:** `pose` (`wave`, `shield`, `cheer`), optional `mood` (`happy`, `worried`), `size` in px (width), `track` to let his eyes follow the pointer, `crop="head"` for the menu and small spots, and a `title` (pass `""` when a word beside him already says who he is).

**Use:**
- One Buddy per screen, near the main idea. `wave` says hello (Home), `shield` means "I'm protecting", `cheer` celebrates a win, `worried` only next to a Not safe answer.
- Keep him at 96px or bigger so a child can read his face; `crop="head"` from 36px.
- He breathes, blinks and waves on his own; all of it stops under Less motion.

**Don't:** recolor him, dress him, give him a different shield, or draw him from scratch. He is an original character; never make him look like a known bear.
