# SpeechBubble

A speech bubble for the child or the toy.

**Provide:** `from` (`child`: sun-soft; `toy`: card), `children` (the words, a string), `status` (`safe`, `careful`, `unsafe`) to color it and stamp a tag on its corner, `typing` for the three thinking dots, `type` to type the words out letter by letter, `onTyped`, and an optional `avatar` (a FamilyArt child).

**Use:** one short sentence, 8 words a line at most. A Not safe bubble shakes once when its tag lands. Screen readers get the whole sentence at once.
