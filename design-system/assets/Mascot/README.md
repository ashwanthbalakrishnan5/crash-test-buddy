# Mascot

Buddy, the crash-test teddy, as standalone SVG files with the colors baked in (for slides, print and anywhere React is not available). In the app, use the `Buddy` component instead: it animates, follows the pointer and respects Less motion.

- `buddy-wave.svg`: waving hello, shield in his other paw. Home screen and greetings.
- `buddy-shield.svg`: holding the Safety Shield in front of him. "I'm protecting this."
- `buddy-cheer.svg`: both arms up. Wins and thank-yous.
- `buddy-head.svg`: head only, for the menu, favicons and small spots (36px and up).

Never recolor, crop through his face, stretch or redraw him.
