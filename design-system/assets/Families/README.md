# Families

Illustrated families of different backgrounds, as SVG files. In the app, use `FamilyArt`.

- `family-mom-kid.svg`: a mom in a hijab and her child.
- `family-dad-kid.svg`: a dad and his daughter.
- `family-grandma-kid.svg`: a grandma and her grandson.

Show a different family on each screen that has people. Never use photos of real children.
