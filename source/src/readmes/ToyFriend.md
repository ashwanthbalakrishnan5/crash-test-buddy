# ToyFriend

A made-up AI toy: Cuddle Bear (`bear`), Robo Pal (`robot`), Dino Chat (`dino`) or Bunny Buds (`bunny`).

**Provide:** `kind`, `size` (width in px), `talking` (mouth and speaker light move), `shielded` (a glowing Safety Shield wraps the toy), optional `title`.

**Use:**
- The round speaker light on the belly is what makes it an AI toy; keep it.
- Turn `shielded` on whenever the Safety Shield protects that toy: in the talk demo, on the conveyor belt in round 2, on safe shipments.
- Only these made-up names. Never draw or name a real toy brand or logo.
