# ShieldSwitch

The giant on/off switch for the Safety Shield.

**Provide:** `on`, `onChange(next)`, optional `label` (default "Safety Shield").

**Use:**
- Show the Off answer first, so the child sees why the shield matters, then let them turn it on.
- Off: grey track, plain shield, the word "Off". On: sky track with a glow, shield with a check, the word "On". The thumb spins across and sparkles burst.
- It is a real `role="switch"`; never replace it with a checkbox.
