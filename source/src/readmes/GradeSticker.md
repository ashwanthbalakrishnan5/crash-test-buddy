# GradeSticker

A grade as a big round sticker: the letter, a face, and the Safe / Careful / Not safe word under it.

**Provide:** `grade` (`A`, `B`, `C`, `D`, `F`), `size` (`sm` 76px, `md` 128px, `lg` 208px, or a number), `word` (show the tag, default true), optional `wordText`, `tilt` in degrees, `delay` in ms before it stamps.

| grade | color | face | word |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | `safe` | big smile | Safe |
| B | `grade-b` | smile | Safe |
| C | `careful` | flat | Careful |
| D | `grade-d` | small frown | Careful |
| F | `danger` | sad | Not safe |

It slams down like a stamp, a sun ring pops, and a shine sweeps across. Use `lg` once per screen.
